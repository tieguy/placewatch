# Miami onboarding log

Log for standing up the Miami account (`mia-wiki-top500.bsky.social`) as the
bot's second city. Written as the work happens; each later launch step gets
appended here too.

## Purpose

Miami is the alpha test for adding cities to sfedits: the first account after
SF, run through the existing bot process and Toolforge tool. Everything that
needs a human here is a scaling cost, so this log is also the source of
requirements for SFED-11 (ranking a platform topic from the database or
dumps) and the self-serve membership-sources web flow ("WikiProject in, bot
out").

## Steps taken

- Approved the design (route A: second account in the running bot) and the
  implementation plan in Plannotator — **operator**; choosing the architecture
  and the non-goals was a judgment call.
- Task 1, per-account secrets for accounts after the first: a config problem
  in such an account disables it with a loud startup log line instead of

- Task 2, posted-log entries record which account posted; one sweeper deletes

  **operator**; every push to `fork/integration` deploys within ~15 minutes,
  so deploys are a human decision. Tasks 1–3 stayed local on `miami-bot`;
  the push and the post-deploy log check moved to launch (Step 7 below).
- WikiProject name and count check with the bot's own
  `fetchProjectArticles({ project: 'Miami', importance: ['Top','High','Mid'] })`:
  **785** articles on 2026-11-21 — **agent**. Expected ≥ 690 (WP 1.0 counted

  `include_subprojects` escape hatch was not needed.
- Added the Miami stanza to `config.base.json` (id `miami`, Bluesky only, no

- Launch (Step 7, pending): get the Bluesky app password from the friend,

  operator's credentials and judgment.

## Ranking alpha (Task 9, 2026-11-21/22 UTC)

The full ranking pipeline ran for Miami on 2026-11-21 (local): `cohort` (2s,
2563 articles), `candidates` (15s, 6802 untagged), `links-prose-all` (126s),
`redirects` (48s), `rank.js` (<1s). No stage needed manual handling — zero
maxlag retries, no interrupts, nothing rerun. Universe: **4033** articles
(2470 tagged + 1563 Wikidata candidates).

**Validation.** Over the n = 1985 tagged articles rated low/mid/high/top,
Pearson r between the human rating (0–3) and canonical inlinks: **0.337**
raw, **0.569** for log1p(inlinks), **0.512** for the inlink percentile rank.
SF's published figure is r = 0.520 (methodology §5.1, transform not
recorded); Miami lands either side of it depending on the transform, so the
measure transfers about as well as it reproduces at home.

**Diff against the live list.** The bot currently watches 785 articles
(WikiProject Top/High/Mid). Switching to the ranked top 500 would **add 183**
and **drop 468**. Of the 1985 rated articles, 1315 (66%) keep their rating
under the proposed tiers, 141 are promoted, 529 demoted.

**Review.** Every entry in "Newly tagged, proposed above Low" (106) and the
top 50 was checked against the Miami-metro question. **No clear mistakes**:
`exclude_titles` is still empty and no rerun was needed. Nine entries are
**doubtful** — their subject is broader than the metro and their claim to a
place is a headquarters or a one-venue connection, which is exactly what the
candidate properties (P159/P276/P39) admit: Univision, UniMás, CONCACAF,
Ion Television, Ion Media (all HQ'd in the metro), FanDuel Sports Network
Florida (statewide, Miami/Tampa/Orlando focus), Miss Florida USA (statewide
pageant), 2026 FIFA World Cup and 2023 World Baseball Classic (Miami one
venue among many). Left in the ranking for the operator to judge.

**Published.** `public/watchlist-miami-500.json` (count 500, a strict prefix
of `watchlist-miami-2500.json`, count 2500), served by the same route loop as
the SF cuts. Not yet pointed at by the bot: switching Miami's
`watchlist_source` to the titles_url is the operator's launch decision.
